# Literature Review — Excerpt (medium)

This curated medium-length excerpt synthesises key themes from the Literature Review, highlighting prior work relevant to co-located PV and battery energy storage systems (BESS) in energy communities.

## Key themes in prior work

- Distributed generation and community energy: increasing interest in community-scale energy systems to increase local renewable penetration and share benefits.
- PV + BESS co-location benefits: improved self-consumption, reduced grid imports, peak shaving, and reduced curtailment.
- Control strategies and operational objectives: literature contrasts maximise self-consumption vs. minimise energy cost under tariffs; rule-based vs optimisation strategies are common.
- Techno-economic assessments: common metrics include NPV, payback, and other cashflow-based KPIs relevant to Chapter 4.
