## Project context

The thesis examines the techno-economic performance of co-located photovoltaic (PV) and battery energy storage systems (BESS) within energy communities. It situates the research in the context of rising distributed renewable generation, increasing interest in community energy schemes, and the need to balance local generation with demand to reduce grid stress, avoid curtailment, and improve local energy resilience. The study seeks to quantify how PV+BESS combinations change energy flows at community scale, and how these changes translate into economic value under realistic cost and tariff assumptions.

## Research questions and objectives

The core research questions focus on: (1) the technical impacts of co-located PV and storage on key operational KPIs (self-consumption, self-sufficiency, reduction in peak import, curtailed generation avoided); (2) the economic outcomes — specifically under what cost, tariff, and incentive conditions does investment in co-located PV+BESS return positive NPV or acceptable payback; and (3) how sensitive results are to assumptions about battery degradation, cost trajectories, and community load profiles. The objective is to combine a high-resolution time-series modelling approach with a scenario-based techno-economic analysis to provide evidence-based recommendations for system sizing and policy design.

## Approach overview

The Introduction outlines a data-driven methodology: combine measured or synthetic high-resolution community load profiles with PV generation scenarios; build a battery operational model capturing state-of-charge dynamics, efficiencies, and realistic operational constraints; run scenario sweeps across PV and battery sizes, tariff regimes and cost assumptions; compute time-series outputs and aggregate economic KPIs (LCOE, NPV, payback). The Introduction also emphasises validation against literature and sensitivity analysis as a route to robust conclusions.

## Expected contributions

The research promises three contributions: a reproducible, data-centric modelling pipeline for assessing PV+BESS at community scale; a set of scenario results that identify technical and economic thresholds for adoption; and policy/design recommendations that align storage sizing and control strategies to local tariff and community load characteristics.
